# 💰 PennyWise

> **Smart, Simple & 100% Private Expense Tracking**

PennyWise is a modern personal finance app designed to help you understand your spending habits without compromising your privacy. No ads, no cloud tracking, and no subscriptions — everything stays securely on your device.

## 🌟 Why PennyWise?

* 🔒 **100% Private & Offline First**  
  Your financial data belongs only to you. PennyWise stores all records locally on your device using IndexedDB. No accounts to create, no servers, and no third-party tracking.
* ⚡ **Lightning Fast & Lightweight**  
  Opens instantly with zero load times. Works smoothly whether you're online or in airplane mode.
* 📱 **Works Everywhere**  
  Install it directly on Android as a standalone app, or use it on iOS and Desktop as an installable Progressive Web App (PWA).

## ✨ Key Features

### 📊 Comprehensive Dashboard
* **Cash Flow Overview:** Real-time visibility into income, expenses, and net balance.
* **Smart Breakdowns:** Interactive category and tag charts (Needs, Wants, Investments, Transfers).
* **Spending Trends:** Daily trendlines and 6-month historical comparisons.
* **Top Payees:** Instant insights into where your money goes most frequently.

### 📝 Frictionless Logging
* **Quick Add:** Type natural entries like `"450 lunch at subway"` to auto-fill category and payee.
* **Payee Rules:** Remembers your past entries and auto-categorizes future expenses from the same merchant.
* **Multi-Account Tracking:** Manage cash, bank accounts, credit cards, debit cards, UPI, and wallets in one place.

### 🎯 Budgets & Recurring Bills
* **Monthly Budgets:** Set spending caps overall or per specific category with visual progress bars.
* **Recurring Transactions:** Track subscriptions, rent, and recurring income with customizable frequencies (weekly, monthly, yearly).

### 📈 In-Depth Reports
* 8 dedicated analytical views: Summary, Income, Expense, Category, Tags, Payment Methods, Merchants, and Budget comparisons.
* Export report summaries directly to CSV anytime.

### 💾 Backup, Export & Restore
* **CSV Export:** Download all transaction history to open in Excel or Google Sheets.
* **Full JSON Backup:** Create a full encrypted backup file of all your data and restore it whenever you switch devices.
* **Bank Statement Import:** Import transactions directly from CSV statement files.

### 🤖 Optional AI Assistant
* Type or paste bank summaries and let AI organize them into structured records.
* Works directly with your own Google Gemini, Groq, OpenAI, or Anthropic API key — keys never leave your phone.

### 🔐 App Lock
* Protect your financial records with a built-in 4 to 6 digit PIN lock.

## 📲 How to Install

### Option 1: Android App (`.apk`)
1. Download the latest **`PennyWise.apk`** to your phone.
2. Tap the downloaded file and select **Install** *(if prompted, enable "Allow from this source")*.
3. Open PennyWise and start tracking immediately — no setup required!

---

### Option 2: Progressive Web App (PWA / iPhone / Desktop)
PennyWise can be installed directly from your browser without using an app store:

* **iPhone / iPad (Safari):**  
  Open the app link -> Tap the **Share** button (box with up arrow) -> Select **"Add to Home Screen"**.
* **Android (Chrome / Brave / Edge):**  
  Open the app link -> Tap the menu `⋮` -> Select **"Install app"** or **"Add to Home screen"**.
* **PC / Mac (Chrome / Edge):**  
  Open the app link -> Click the **Install (🖥️)** icon in the address bar.

## 🛡️ Privacy Guarantee

PennyWise is built with a zero-knowledge architecture:
* **No Server Storage:** We do not operate remote databases for your transactions.
* **No Telemetry / Analytics:** Your usage data, spending amounts, and notes are never sent over the internet.
* **Your Data is Yours:** Export your complete data history at any time with a single tap.
